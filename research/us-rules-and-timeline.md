# Immigrant (game): US immigration rules and 2015-2026 timeline

Compiled 2026-11-08. Game-design reference only, not legal advice. Neutral and factual.

## How to read the labels

- **CONFIRMED** = read on a primary source (USCIS, EOIR/DOJ, DHS/OHSS, State Dept, Federal Register) during this research session.
- **LIKELY** = reported by a secondary source (law firm, university, news, think tank) that I could not match to a primary page. Several 2026 items fall here because many primary sites blocked or returned 404 to the fetch tool (travel.state.gov returned a Cloudflare block; some uscis.gov and federalregister.gov URLs failed).
- **UNVERIFIED** = not checked this session. "UNVERIFIED (stable rule)" means a long-standing statute or regulation I know from background knowledge and did not re-fetch. The URL beside it is where to confirm. Treat all of those as needing a check before shipping numbers to players.

Today's date for "current" statements: 2026-11-08. Many 2026 rules are in active litigation, so "current" can change in days.

## 1. Status rules the game needs

### 1.1 B-1/B-2 visitor
- Typical admission up to 6 months; extension via I-539. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.uscis.gov/forms/all-forms/how-do-i-extend-my-stay-as-a-b-visitor
- No work authorization; status change to F-1/H-1B etc. possible via I-539/I-129 but "preconceived intent" is a risk. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers

### 1.2 F-1 student
- Admission is for "duration of status" (D/S), tied to the I-20 program end, plus a 60-day post-completion period. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov
- **2026 change:** DHS published a final rule on July 17, 2026 replacing D/S with fixed admission periods for F, J and I nonimmigrants, effective Sept 15, 2026. LIKELY: https://www.faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2026/7/dhs-final-rule-ending-duration-of-status-effective-september-15-2026
- **Court status:** a federal court in Massachusetts postponed the effective date nationwide on Sept 14, 2026, one day before it took effect; D/S remains in place while litigation continues. LIKELY: https://www.cozen.com/news-resources/publications/2026/federal-court-blocks-dhs-rule-ending-duration-of-status- and https://www.aip.org/fyi/court-temporarily-blocks-rule-ending-duration-of-status-for-international-students-september-17-2026. I could not confirm status after late September 2026.
- **CPT** (curricular practical training): authorized by the school (DSO); 12+ months of full-time CPT eliminates OPT eligibility. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/students/work/curricular-practical-training
- **Post-completion OPT:** up to 12 months; 90 total days of unemployment allowed. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.ice.gov/sevis/practical-training
- **STEM OPT extension:** 24 more months for eligible STEM degrees with an E-Verify employer; 150 total days unemployment across OPT + STEM OPT (i.e. 60 extra). UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.ice.gov/sevis/practical-training
- **Cap-gap:** a timely filed H-1B change of status extends F-1/OPT work authorization to April 1 of the fiscal year (2024 modernization rule). UNVERIFIED (stable rule; the April 1 date comes from the Jan 2025-effective H-1B modernization rule). https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/optional-practical-training
- **OPT policy risk 2026:** In Jan 2026 DHS said it is "re-evaluating the scope and duration" of OPT/STEM OPT; ICE agenda item "Practical Training" (RIN 1653-AA97) targets a 02/2027 NPRM with no text yet. LIKELY: https://www.f1jobs.io/resources/blog/is-opt-ending-what-dhs-proposed-october-2026
- **OPT fee proposal:** a proposed rule "Optional Practical Training Fees" (91 FR 64566, Oct 8, 2026) would impose $70,000 (initial OPT) and $30,000 (subsequent). Comments due Nov 9, 2026. Not in effect. Who pays (school vs student) is disputed between sources. This figure looks extraordinary and I could only find it through a summary of AILA/NAFSA pages: LIKELY, treat cautiously. https://www.nafsa.org/optional-practical-training-fee-rule-portal and https://www.aila.org/library/dhs-to-post-notice-of-proposed-rulemaking-on-optional-practical-training-fees

### 1.3 J-1 exchange visitor
- Duration set by DS-2019 per category; many categories carry the 2-year home residency requirement (212(e)). Grace period 30 days after program end. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://j1visa.state.gov
- Covered by the same July 2026 D/S rule and injunction as F-1 (see 1.2). LIKELY (sources above).

### 1.4 H-1B
- **Cap (CONFIRMED):** 65,000 new per fiscal year plus 20,000 for those with a US master's degree or higher. Cap-exempt: higher-ed institutions, affiliated nonprofits, nonprofit/government research orgs. https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/h-1b-specialty-occupations
- **6-year limit (CONFIRMED):** initial up to 3 years, extendable to 6 total. Extensions beyond 6 years available in certain green card situations (AC21: pending PERM/I-140 for 365+ days, or approved I-140 with a backlogged priority date, in 1-year or 3-year increments; the AC21 detail is UNVERIFIED (stable rule), https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/permanent-workers). Same USCIS page above for the 6-year text.
- **60-day grace (CONFIRMED):** after employment ends, up to 60 consecutive days or until the validity period ends if shorter, to find a new employer, change status or leave. Same USCIS page.
- **Registration (CONFIRMED):** first used in the FY2021 season (March 2020); FY2027 registration ran noon ET March 4 to noon ET March 19, 2026; registration fee $215. https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/alerts/fy-2027-h-1b-cap-initial-registration-period-opens-on-march-4. Before registration (FY2016-FY2020 seasons) employers filed full petitions in a first-5-business-day April window and USCIS ran a lottery.
- **Weighted selection (rule LIKELY, USCIS alert CONFIRMED):** DHS final rule published Dec 29, 2025 (Federal Register doc 2025-23853), effective Feb 27, 2026, used for FY2027. Registrations get entries by the OEWS wage level of the offered job: Level IV = 4 entries, III = 3, II = 2, I = 1. USCIS alert confirms weighted selection prioritizing "higher-skilled and higher-paid aliens" and that selection is by unique beneficiary. Entry weights: LIKELY, https://www.niwus.com/resources/fy-2027-h-1b-cap-registration-changes ; rule: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/12/29/2025-23853/weighted-selection-process-for-registrants-and-petitioners-seeking-to-file-cap-subject-h-1b (fetch redirected; text not read).

### 1.5 H-4 and H-4 EAD
- H-4 dependents (spouse, unmarried children under 21) of H-1B. H-4 EAD available for spouses of H-1B holders with approved I-140 or who are in AC21 6th-year-plus extensions; rule effective May 26, 2015. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/h-4-employment-authorization
- Age-out at 21 for H-4 children is a known game-relevant cliff. UNVERIFIED (stable rule).

### 1.6 L-1
- L-1A (managers/executives) max 7 years; L-1B (specialized knowledge) max 5 years; one year continuous employment abroad in prior 3; L-2 spouses are work-authorized. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/l-1a-intracompany-transferee-executive-or-manager
- L-1 employers also covered by the 2026 9-11 fee (see 1.4). CONFIRMED via USCIS H-1B page.

### 1.7 TN (USMCA professionals)
- Canadian and Mexican citizens in listed professions; 3-year increments, no overall cap on renewals. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/tn-nafta-professionals

### 1.8 H-2A / H-2B
- H-2A agricultural: temporary need generally up to 1 year per approval, 3-year maximum stay. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). H-2B nonagricultural: annual cap 66,000 (33,000 per half-year), with Congress/DHS supplemental visas in recent years. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/h-2b-temporary-non-agricultural-workers
- H-2A has no numerical cap. UNVERIFIED (stable rule).

### 1.9 O-1
- Extraordinary ability: initial up to 3 years, then 1-year extensions, no annual cap. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/o-1-visa-individuals-with-extraordinary-ability-or-achievement

### 1.10 Employment-based green cards (EB)
- Annual EB limit about 140,000 plus unused family numbers; 7% per-country cap on preference visas (INA 202). UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.uscis.gov/green-card/green-card-eligibility/green-card-for-employment-based-immigrants
- **EB-1** (extraordinary ability, outstanding researchers, multinational managers); **EB-2** (advanced degree or exceptional ability, incl. **NIW**, which needs no job offer or PERM); **EB-3** (skilled, professionals, other workers); **EB-5** (investor, $800,000 TEA or $1,050,000 standard minimum since 2022 reform). UNVERIFIED (stable rule; EB-5 amounts from the March 2022 reform act, not re-fetched). https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/permanent-workers/eb-5-immigrant-investor-program
- PERM labor certification generally precedes EB-2/EB-3 (non-NIW). UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/programs/permanent

### 1.12 Family-based

### 1.13 Marriage-based green cards
- Marriage under 2 years old at approval -> 2-year conditional residence (CR1/CR6); file I-751 jointly in the 90 days before the card expires; a receipt notice extends status while pending (extension length varies by period; not verified). UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.uscis.gov/green-card/after-we-grant-your-green-card/conditions-on-permanent-residence-based-on-marriage
- Median USCIS processing times, family-based I-485 and I-130 immediate relative, see 3.3 table (CONFIRMED, FY2016-FY2024).
- K-1 fiance: 90 days to marry after entry, then adjust. UNVERIFIED (stable rule).

### 1.14 Diversity Visa (DV)
- Statutory 50,000 per year, from countries with low recent immigration; free entry, short registration window each autumn; selection is not a visa. LIKELY: https://www.immi-usa.com/green-card-diversity-visa-lottery/
- **Dec 18, 2025:** President announced suspension of the DV program after a campus shooting; State stopped issuing DV visas Dec 23, 2025. Sources vary on mechanism. LIKELY: https://www.cozen.com/news-resources/publications/2025/green-card-lottery-program-suspended and https://eiglaw.com/?p=14073
- **DV-2027:** entry period not announced as of late Oct 2026; two law-firm sources disagree on a court-ordered reopening (Oct 14 vs 30 days from Nov 1, 2026). UNVERIFIED which, if either, happened. https://www.gozellaw.com/blog/dv-lottery-2027 , https://www.visalawyerblog.com/federal-judge-orders-reopening-of-2027-diversity-visa-lottery-registration-by-november-1st/

### 1.15 Adjustment of status vs consular processing
- Adjustment (I-485) from inside the US requires lawful inspection and admission (or parole) and, for most, maintaining status; immediate relatives are forgiven many status violations. Consular processing (DS-260, interview abroad) triggers the unlawful presence bars on departure. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.uscis.gov/green-card/green-card-processes-and-procedures/adjustment-of-status
- 2026: State paused immigrant visa issuance to nationals of 75 countries (announced Jan 14, effective Jan 21, 2026) citing public charge; reports say it ended Aug 21, 2026; one low-quality source claims a court vacated it in Oct 2026 (UNVERIFIED). LIKELY: https://www.dorsey.com/newsresources/publications/client-alerts/2026/1/immigrant-visas-for-75-countries-on-pause , https://ariasvilla.com/75-country-immigrant-visa-pause-is-no-longer-in-effect/
- Separate USCIS hold on benefits for people connected to 39 designated countries; reportedly subject to court orders. LIKELY: https://www.breakthroughusa.com/uscis-39-country-hold-75-visa-pause/

### 1.16 Public charge history
- 1999 interim field guidance governed for about two decades. CONFIRMED (archived USCIS page): https://www.uscis.gov/green-card/green-card-processes-and-procedures/public-charge
- **2019 rule:** published Aug 14, 2019, effective Feb 24, 2020 after a Supreme Court stay lifted injunctions (dates UNVERIFIED, from background). USCIS (CONFIRMED) says it has "not been in effect since March 9, 2021."
- **2022 rule:** effective Dec 23, 2022, applies to adjustment applications submitted on/after that date; Policy Manual updated Dec 19, 2022. CONFIRMED (same USCIS page). Only cash assistance and long-term institutionalization count.
- **2025-2026:** proposed Nov 2025; **final rule rescinding the 2022 regulation published July 20, 2026, effective Sept 18, 2026**, applying to adjustment applications submitted on/after that date, with a new Form I-485 and a case-by-case discretionary framework; officers can weigh receipt of any means-tested benefit. LIKELY: https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/2806/2026-07-20-dhs-rescinds-2022-public-charge-regulation-favor-policy , https://www.ey.com/en_ca/insights/law/mobility-alerts/2026/us-rescinds-2022-public-charge-regulations

### 1.17 Unlawful presence bars and waivers
- 180 days to <1 year unlawful presence then departure = 3-year bar; 1 year or more then departure = 10-year bar (INA 212(a)(9)(B)). UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.uscis.gov/laws-and-policy/legislation/immigration-and-nationality-act
- Unlawful presence does not accrue for most F/J/H holders in authorized D/S periods until a formal finding. UNVERIFIED (stable rule).
- **I-601A provisional waiver** (since March 2013; expanded Aug 2016 to all with qualifying USC/LPR spouse or parent hardship) lets some apply before leaving for consular interview. UNVERIFIED (stable rule). https://www.uscis.gov/i-601a
- Permanent bar (212(a)(9)(C)) for unlawful presence over 1 year then re-entry without inspection. UNVERIFIED (stable rule).

### 1.18 Out-of-status consequences (game-relevant)
- Status violation can bar adjustment (for employment/preference categories; 245(c) exceptions, 245(k) 180-day grace for employment-based). UNVERIFIED (stable rule).
- H-1B workers: 60-day grace on job loss (CONFIRMED, 1.4). F-1: 60-day post-completion; OPT unemployment day limits (1.2). Unauthorized employment ends F-1 status (UNVERIFIED stable rule).
