# India to US Migration: Game Reference for "Immigrant"

Compiled 2026-11-08. Game-design reference, not legal advice.
Labels: CONFIRMED = read in a source page this session, or stated identically by multiple sources. LIKELY = reported by secondary sources or well-known rule not re-read at the primary page. UNVERIFIED = could not be checked; do not show to players as fact.
Research limits: the MPI April 2026 page returned HTTP 403 and the DHS FY2023 LPR PDF was unreadable by the fetch tool, so several primary tables were NOT read. Many law-firm/news sources are secondary. All gaps are listed in section 5.

Diversity note for writers: Indian immigrants are not one profile. Home languages in the US are led by Hindi, Telugu, Gujarati, Tamil (Pew, below); people arrive from every state and union territory; education runs from doctorates to high school; jobs run from engineers to motel owners to nurses to cab/trucking/retail workers. Pew counts 23% of Indian Americans without a bachelor's degree. Avoid "all Indians are tech workers."

## 2. TEN CAREER PATHS

General status rules used below (LIKELY unless noted; verify at uscis.gov and travel.state.gov/visabulletin before shipping):
- H-1B: 3-year initial, extendable to 6 years total. Beyond 6: 1-year extensions if a PERM or I-140 was filed 365+ days earlier, or 3-year extensions if I-140 approved and priority date not current (AC21 sections 104(c)/106). Source discussion: https://www.rnlawgroup.com/options-for-an-h-1b-employee-with-approved-i-140-that-has-been-laid-off/
- 60-day grace period after job loss (8 CFR 214.1(l)(2)) or until I-94 end if shorter; must find a new sponsor, change status (e.g. to H-4 via I-539), or leave. Travel abroad ends it. LIKELY. https://www.immihelp.com/h-1b-visa-layoff-and-60-days-grace-period/
- H-4 spouse EAD only if H-1B holder has approved I-140 or AC21 extension. LIKELY (same sources). Spouse is otherwise barred from work for years.
- Cross-chargeability: a spouse born in another country can sometimes be the primary chargeable applicant. LIKELY, rule knowledge.
- Per-country cap: 7% of employment-based numbers per country, which creates the India backlog. CONFIRMED rule; effect quantified in Cato (section 1.2).
- Visa Bulletin snapshot November 2026: EB-2 India Final Action Date 1 Nov 2013; EB-3 India 22 Jan 2014; Dates for Filing 15 Jan 2015 for EB-2 and EB-3; EB-1 India 1 Feb 2023; EB-2 India was "unavailable" in the closing months of FY2026. LIKELY (law-firm trackers, not the bulletin itself). https://www.beyondborderglobal.com/resources/november-2026-visa-bulletin ; https://www.f1jobs.io/resources/blog/november-2026-visa-bulletin-explained-eb2-eb3-india . Reading: an Indian EB-2 applicant with a priority date in 2013 is only now at the front, i.e. a 13-year gap. For game timing: EB-2/EB-3 India for someone filing in 2026 is UNVERIFIED but plausibly 10 to 20+ years, possibly more; EB-1 is about 3 years behind; use ranges, not point claims.

Pay ranges: ONLY one BLS metro figure was verified (San Jose software developer mean $234,540, year not confirmed, https://blsmon1.bls.gov/oes/current/oes151252.htm). NYC software developer median about $161,970 (May 2024, third-party site, https://wage-950.pages.dev/salary/software-developer/new-york/new-york-newark-jersey-city-ny-nj ); NYC May 2022 mean $142,830 (BLS, https://www.bls.gov/oes/2022/may/oes151252.htm). National software developer median about $133,080 (May 2024, third party). All other ranges below are DESIGN ESTIMATES (UNVERIFIED) and must be calibrated with BLS OEWS before release. The overall Indian-American full-time median of $106,400 (Pew, CONFIRMED) is a sanity anchor.

### Path 1. Software engineer (employer-sponsored)
- Status ladder: F-1 or direct hire abroad then H-1B (lottery) then PERM/I-140 EB-2 or EB-3 then I-485 then green card then N-400 citizen. LIKELY.
- Time by arrival year: 2015-2018 arrivals: H-1B 6+ years, green card often 2023-2030 for EB-2/EB-3 India; citizenship +5 years. 2019 onward: expect H-1B extensions beyond 6 years and a green card in the 2030s to 2040s on the backlog; EB-1/NIW or a foreign-born spouse can cut it sharply. UNVERIFIED ranges; direction CONFIRMED by bulletin data above.
- Employers/cities: big tech, finance, startups; Bay Area, Seattle, Dallas, NYC/NJ, Austin. Metro counts: Pew top metros above.
- Pay (design estimate, UNVERIFIED except San Jose anchor): entry $110k-160k; mid $160k-250k; senior $250k-400k+ total comp in SF Bay; Dallas and NYC lower base. 
- Risks: layoffs and 60-day clock; lottery loss (weighted lottery 2026 favors higher wage level); $100k fee for overseas hires; backlog; child age-out; spouse barred from work without EAD.
- Milestones: H-1B approved, I-140 approved (portability), spouse EAD, green card, home purchase (Indian-headed homeownership 62%), citizenship.

### Path 2. Data/ML engineer or scientist
- Ladder: often MS or PhD (F-1), OPT/STEM OPT (up to 3 years total), H-1B, EB-2 (advanced degree) or EB-1A/NIW if publications. PhD route can use EB-1 where the India date is shorter. LIKELY.
- Time: similar to Path 1; EB-1/NIW case: green card possibly 2 to 6 years from filing under the current EB-1 date, UNVERIFIED.
- Employers: AI labs, big tech, healthcare analytics, finance; Bay Area, Seattle, Boston, Pittsburgh.
- Pay: design estimate entry $130k-190k; senior $250k-500k; research-lab outliers higher. UNVERIFIED.
- Risks: model of hiring cycles, layoffs, lottery, policy changes; O-1/EB-1A rejection risk.
- Milestones: publication/patent record, NIW approval, green card, founding or leading a team.

### Path 3. Product manager
- Ladder: F-1 MBA or MS then OPT/H-1B, or intracompany transfer; EB-2 (MBA/MS) PERM. LIKELY.
- Time: same backlog problem as Path 1; PM roles can be harder to classify as "specialty occupation," so more H-1B denial/RFE risk. UNVERIFIED.
- Employers: tech platforms, SaaS, fintech; SF, Seattle, NYC, Austin.
- Pay: design estimate entry $130k-180k, mid $180k-300k, director $300k+ total comp. UNVERIFIED.
- Risks: layoffs hit PMs; H-1B cap lottery; role-code scrutiny.
- Milestones: sponsor, promotion to senior/group PM, green card, VP or founder move.

### Path 4. IT services/consulting worker (client-site)
- Ladder: L-1 intracompany transfer (after 1 year at the Indian affiliate) or H-1B via a services firm, PERM EB-2/EB-3, long backlog; L-1A managers can go EB-1C (multinational manager), a faster route for some. LIKELY.
- Time: L-1 up to 5 (specialized knowledge) or 7 (manager) years; EB-1C India date is the EB-1 chart. UNVERIFIED years.
- Employers: Indian-based IT firms and US consultancies (the Indian-based firms' initial H-1B approvals fell about 70% FY2015 to FY2025, see 1.2); clusters in NJ/NYC, Dallas, Atlanta, Chicago, Charlotte.
- Pay: design estimate entry $70k-95k, mid $95k-140k, senior/manager $140k-200k. UNVERIFIED.
- Risks: client contract loss ("bench"), worksite scrutiny, wage-level H-1B weighting hurts lower-wage roles, layoffs, offshoring pressure.
- Milestones: transfer to direct hire at a US firm, EB-1C or PERM, relocation to a lower-cost city, green card.

### Path 5. MS student to OPT to H-1B
- Ladder: F-1 (visa interview in India) then OPT (12 months) plus STEM OPT (24 months) then H-1B lottery up to 3 tries (cap registrations) then PERM. India sent 363,019 students 2024-25 (see 1.2). LIKELY. A cited study finds about one third of Indian-born US graduates leave the US. LIKELY. https://www.demogr.mpg.de/en/publications_databases_6118/publications_1904/journal_articles/greener_pastures_why_indian_international_students_leave_the_us_labor_market_8249
- Time: 2 years study + up to 3 years OPT; cap-gap and lottery gate: arrival 2015-2018 had random lottery; 2026 onward weighted by wage, $100k fee not applicable to in-US change of status. LIKELY.
- Employers: any sponsor-friendly firm; Texas, California, NJ, Illinois, Massachusetts (top student states per Open Doors).
- Pay: tuition and living cost is large (student debt, amounts not verified); first job design estimate $80k-140k. UNVERIFIED.
- Risks: lottery loss means leaving or a second master's/other visa (cap-exempt employers, O-1); OPT policy proposals (a 2025 bill to eliminate OPT, outcome UNVERIFIED); unemployment days limit on OPT. 
- Milestones: admission, internship, STEM OPT, H-1B selected, I-140.

### Path 6. Physician (IMG)
- Ladder: USMLE steps, ECFMG certification, residency match, J-1 (ECFMG) or H-1B for residency; after J-1 a 2-year home-residence requirement unless waived (Conrad 30 / underserved-area service); then H-1B and EB-2 NIW (physician national interest waiver) or EB-2. LIKELY rule knowledge. 
- Facts: about 23% of actively licensed IMGs in 2016 trained in India (nearly 50,000 of 210,000+). LIKELY, dated. https://www.voanews.com/amp/student-union_doctors-india-frustrated-visa-issues/6183317.html . Indian-born immigrant physician count about 62,800 (2024 ACS via secondary source). UNVERIFIED. 
- Time: 3 to 7 years residency/fellowship, then 3 years waiver service; green card often 6-10+ years after arrival; India backlog affects EB-2; physician NIW may use the EB-2 chart. UNVERIFIED ranges.
- Employers/cities: community hospitals in underserved areas (Midwest, Appalachia, Texas), academic centers; widely dispersed rather than only coastal.
- Pay: residency about $60k-80k; attending $250k-450k+ varying by specialty and region. UNVERIFIED (check BLS physician data).
- Risks: match failure, J-1 waiver denial or no waiver job, service-area location constraints, visa delays for Step exams/interviews.
- Milestones: match, residency completion, waiver job, board certification, green card, private practice or partnership.

### Path 7. Nurse or pharmacist
- Ladder: nursing: NCLEX + credential evaluation, then EB-3 (Schedule A shortage occupation, no PERM labor test) from abroad. Pharmacists: US PharmD/foreign pharmacist then H-1B or EB-2/EB-3. LIKELY (Schedule A includes registered nurses). EB-3 India backlog still applies (EB-3 India date 22 Jan 2014 per November 2026 bulletin). 
- Time: for an RN arriving via EB-3 India, UNVERIFIED but plausibly a decade+ wait for a number; many nurses instead come via family routes or via other nationalities' routes. Indian nurse counts in US: NOT FOUND. UNVERIFIED.
- Employers/cities: hospitals and long-term care in Texas, New York/New Jersey, California, Florida, Illinois.
- Pay: RN design estimate $70k-120k depending on state; pharmacist $120k-150k. UNVERIFIED; verify via BLS OEWS 29-1141, 29-1051.
- Risks: staffing agency and recruiter exploitation, contract penalties for leaving, backlog, licensing delays, credential recognition.
- Milestones: license, green card, advanced degree (NP), sponsoring family.

### Path 8. Accountant/finance professional
- Ladder: US CPA exams (as foreign graduate) or Big 4/finance employer sponsored H-1B, or MBA/MS in Finance then OPT/H-1B; EB-2/EB-3. LIKELY. 
- Employers: banks, Big 4, consulting, asset managers; NYC/NJ, Chicago, Dallas, Charlotte, Bay Area.
- Pay: design estimate entry $70k-100k; senior manager $150k-250k; investment-banking higher. UNVERIFIED.
- Risks: H-1B lottery, role may not qualify as specialty occupation (RFEs), CPA licensing differences by state, layoffs during finance downturns.
- Milestones: CPA license, promotion to manager/director, green card, CFO/controller role.

### Path 9. Small-business owner (motel/hotel, convenience/retail)
- Ladder: often arrives via family immigration (F-4 siblings/E-2 not available to Indian nationals, which is CONFIRMED rule: India is not an E-2 treaty country; verify at travel.state.gov) or EB-5 (investor). Many owners are LPRs/citizens who started as hotel employees; others EB-5. LIKELY.
- Facts: AAHOA reports its members own about 60% of US hotels (34,260 hotels, 2025 study); industry-sourced, not independently verified. LIKELY. https://www.americanbazaaronline.com/?p=446674 . The old "Patel" surname statistic is decades old; do not cite it as current. UNVERIFIED.
- Time: Family-based (F4 sibling) wait for India is very long (UNVERIFIED years); EB-5 India has a backlog (UNVERIFIED).
- Cities: spread nationwide, smaller towns and highway corridors; Georgia, Texas, California, Florida, the Midwest.
- Pay/economics: income varies widely; design only: financing and mortgage obligations, revenue swings with occupancy. UNVERIFIED.
- Risks: loan default, recession/pandemic occupancy hit, franchise terms, family labor strain, crime/insurance costs.
- Milestones: first property, second property, franchise, paying off mortgage, next generation takes over or goes to professional school.

- Facts: NFAP (April 2026 data) counts 96 of the 775 US unicorns as having India-born founders, the top country; immigrants founded 455 of 775 (59%). LIKELY. https://www.business-standard.com/immigration/india-born-founders-behind-96-us-unicorns-highest-among-immigrants-study-126060500076_1.html ; https://thequint.com/us-nri-news/india-top-country-of-origin-for-immigrant-founders-of-us-unicorns-nfap-study . ## 4. TWENTY CHALLENGE IDEAS

Each lists the grounding fact (label) and pass/fail. Use "Year" in the game to switch rules by arrival cohort.

1. H-1B lottery. Basis: cap lottery; weighted by wage level for FY2027 (LIKELY). Pass: selected (higher odds with higher wage level or in-country filing); Fail: not selected, must change plan or leave.
